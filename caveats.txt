CAVEATS
=======

Module: Admin Dashboard - Total Revenue
Backend: getDashboardStats
File: backend/controllers/orderController.js
Lines: 1301-1316

Total Revenue is calculated as:

  order sales + installment downpayments + recorded installment payments

- Orders marked "completed" or "paid" add their full totalPrice.
- Approved installment applications add their downPayment.
- Every entry in an application's paymentHistory is added again.

Caveat:
When an installment-linked order is later marked "completed", its
totalPrice (totalRepayment) can also be counted. This means the same
financing deal can be double-counted through both the linked order and
the application's downpayment/paymentHistory.

Refunds are also not subtracted from Total Revenue.

Impact:
Admin Dashboard Total Revenue and the All-time revenue card may overstate
actual money collected for installment sales.
